    Always the fastest server

    Every minute this server checks that both tunnels answer. One that stops answering moves at once to the fastest server that works. A working one moves when a server at least a quarter (and 20 ms) faster turns up, measured every few hours, so the Wi-Fi is not paused for small gains. HomeTube's tunnel also moves when YouTube refuses to answer through it, and a new server only counts once YouTube answers. The two tunnels never share a server.

    YouTube on the TV

    Open /tv on the TV for HomeTube, YouTube for the TV. Connecting an account adds your own subscriptions, recommendations and Watch Later.

    Checking…
    Google can flag or lock an account it suddenly sees from another country, and the VPN moves between servers. So videos never use the account: only its own lists are fetched signed in, by a separate service that alone holds the cookies, and they go out one fixed way, this board's own internet connection by default, where you use the account at home anyway. To connect: open a private browser window, sign in at youtube.com, export its cookies with the open-source "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" extension, close the private window at once (so Google does not rotate the session), then choose the file here. Only YouTube and Google cookies are kept.
